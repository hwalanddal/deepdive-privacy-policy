Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 12, 2026  |  Last Updated: July 12, 2026

Hwalanddal (the "Company"), the provider of DeepDive (the "Service"), values the privacy of its users and complies with applicable laws and regulations, including the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea. Through this Privacy Policy, the Company informs users of the purposes and methods for which personal information provided by users is used and the measures taken to protect such personal information.

1. Types of Personal Information Collected and Methods of Collection

A. Information Collected

The Service does not have a registration process and therefore does not collect personal information directly entered by users, such as names or email addresses. However, the following information may be automatically generated and collected during the use of the Service.

Category Information Collected
Information automatically collected during use of the Service Device identification information (advertising identifiers such as ADID/IDFA, device model, operating system type and version), app version, app launch and usage records, time spent on each screen, click and interaction logs, error (crash) logs, and IP address
When contacting customer support Email address and inquiry details (only when using the inquiry function)

※ The information above is automatically collected through analytics tools such as Google Analytics for Firebase / Firebase Crashlytics and does not include information such as names or contact details that can directly identify a specific individual.

B. Methods of Collection

  • Automatically generated and collected during app installation and use of the Service (through analytics SDKs)
  • Collected only when voluntarily entered by the user when using customer support or the inquiry function

2. Purposes of Collecting and Using Personal Information

  • Analysis of Service Usage Statistics: To understand which features are frequently used and how users interact with the Service, and to use such information to improve the Service
  • Ensuring Service Stability: To identify and resolve the causes of errors (crashes) and prevent abnormal use of the Service
  • Service Development and Feature Improvements: To plan new features and improve the user experience (UX)
  • Customer Support: To review and respond to inquiries (when the inquiry function is used)

※ The Service does not use personal information for the purpose of displaying advertisements.

3. Retention and Use Period of Personal Information

The Company shall destroy personal information without undue delay once the purpose for collecting and using such information has been fulfilled. Automatically collected analytics information will either be destroyed after being retained for the periods specified below, or retained only in a statistically processed form that cannot be used to identify an individual.

Information Retained Basis/Purpose of Retention Retention Period
App usage analytics logs and crash logs Service quality improvement Up to 14 months (subject to the policies of the analytics tools)
Customer inquiry records Management of customer inquiry and complaint history 1 year after the inquiry has been resolved
Access logs (including IP addresses) Protection of Communications Secrets Act 3 months

4. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

In principle, the Company does not provide users' personal information to external third parties. However, exceptions may apply in the following cases:

  • When the user has given prior consent to the provision of personal information to a third party
  • When required by applicable laws or regulations, or when an investigative agency requests such information in accordance with the procedures and methods prescribed by law for investigative purposes

5. Entrustment of Personal Information Processing

The Company entrusts certain personal information processing activities to external service providers for the purposes of analyzing Service usage statistics and improving errors and stability. The Company establishes and manages the necessary requirements to ensure that entrusted service providers process personal information securely.

Entrusted Service Provider Entrusted Services
Google (Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics) Analysis of app usage statistics and collection and analysis of error (crash) logs

6. User Rights and How to Exercise Them

  • The Service does not have a registration process and therefore does not provide separate functions for viewing or modifying account information.
  • Users may reset their advertising identifier or restrict the use of data for personalized advertising or analytics purposes through "Settings > Privacy > Advertising" or "Google Account Settings" on their device.
  • If you wish to access or delete your personal information, please contact the person responsible for personal information protection by email using the contact information below, and the Company will take appropriate action without undue delay.

7. Procedures and Methods for Destruction of Personal Information

  • Destruction Procedure: Collected information is transferred to a separate database after the purpose of collection has been fulfilled, stored for a certain period in accordance with internal policies and applicable laws, and then destroyed.
  • Method of Destruction: Personal information stored in electronic file format is deleted using technical methods that prevent the records from being restored or recovered.

8. Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information

  • Minimization of personnel handling personal information and relevant training
  • Restriction of access rights to collected information
  • Management in accordance with the security policies and data processing agreements of analytics tool providers such as Google
  • Implementation of technical measures to protect against hacking and other security threats

9. Protection of Children's Personal Information

The Service is not primarily intended for children under the age of 14 and does not directly collect personal information from children through a registration process, as no registration process is provided. However, the Company complies with applicable laws and regulations with respect to automatically collected device and usage information.

10. Operation of Advertising Identifiers and Analytics Tools (SDKs)

Although the Service does not display advertisements, it uses analytics tools (SDKs), including Google Analytics for Firebase, to collect advertising identifiers (ADID/IDFA) and usage statistics for the purpose of improving the Service. Users may reset their advertising identifiers or restrict the collection of analytics data through their device settings. Such restrictions may affect the collection of usage statistics but will not restrict the user's ability to use the Service itself.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy shall take effect from the effective date stated above. If any additions, deletions, or corrections are made to this Privacy Policy due to changes in applicable laws or Company policies, the Company will notify users of such changes through notices at least 7 days before the changes take effect.
